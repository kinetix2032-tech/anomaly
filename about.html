THE BEGINNING

ANOMALY started with a simple idea: make something I would actually wear.

I was inspired by a friend who had started his own clothing brand, and I wanted to follow through and create something of my own — but on my terms.

Something that sounded right. Something that meant something to me. Something that reflected how I saw myself.

The name ANOMALY came from a combination of the sound, what it meant, and how it described who I am.

At some point, I had to understand that I was different. Not in the conventional way. I didn't fit every expected pattern, and eventually I stopped seeing that as something that needed to be fixed.

POINT OF ORIGIN

ANOMALY is rooted in Zambia.

Where we come from matters. But it doesn't have to define how far we can go.

The goal is to carry that origin with us while building something that can exist anywhere in the world.
